# Booking deposit
Say `Table for 6 Saturday 8pm`, or call MCP `book_table`.

- 6 or more guests: Sally creates a Stripe Payment Link for £10/head (H1). Pay with `4242 4242 4242 4242`.
- Under 6: confirmed, no deposit.
- Mentions an allergy: handed to staff (H2).

See it: the record page → **Payment link**, then **Receipt** once paid.
